How It Works
1
Build your profile
Add your skills, links and a short introduction. Mentors read this first.
2
Apply to programs
Up to three applications per term. Each one is reviewed by the program mentors.
3
Get matched
Mentors score applicants on skills fit and intent, then accept for the term.
4
Do the work
Twelve weeks of scoped tasks with weekly reviews from your mentor.
5
Graduate
Pass the final evaluation and receive your stipend installments.
Who Can Apply
You are 18 or older.
You can commit consistent hours for the full term.
You may have up to 3 applications at a time.
You may only enter one Mentorship program in your career.
Not every program is funded. Where a stipend is offered, the amount varies by program and by your country of residence. Each program page lists its own stipend.
Funded work where available
Many programs carry a stipend, paid in milestone installments. Amounts vary by program and country of residence.
A named mentor
You work with an experienced project contributor who scopes your tasks and reviews everything you submit.
Real, merged work
Tasks come from the project backlog, so what you finish ships to the people who depend on it.
No experience required
Most programs expect you to learn the domain as you start. Prior open source work is not required.
Frequently Asked
Full FAQ & documentation
Is there a stipend?
Not every program is funded. Where a stipend is offered it varies by program and by your country of residence, and the program page says so before you apply.
Who can apply?
Anyone 18 or older who has not previously completed an LFX mentorship.
What happens at the end?
You complete a final evaluation with your mentor. Graduating is recorded on your profile.
How much time does a term take?
A significant weekly commitment across a multi-week program. Each program page lists its own term dates.
Do I need to be a student?
No. Some programs ask for school enrollment verification, but many do not.
How many programs can I apply to?
You may have up to three applications at a time. Withdrawing an application frees a slot.
How are applicants chosen?
Program mentors review each application against the skills they listed and what you wrote in your cover letter.
